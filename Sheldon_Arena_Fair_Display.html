Team Formation

Play openly in Terms 1 & 2 — then register your official 5-player team for the league!
🛡️
Lineup
1. Full Team of 5
  • Decided by the end of Term 2.
  • Must be a complete team of 5 players from your year group.
  • All students must be part of a team for league matches.
  • Appoint 1 Team Captain to lead strategy and register match lineups.
🏷️
Identity
2. Team Name & 3-Letter Tag
  • Create a unique squad name (e.g. Chippenham Knights, Apex Titans).
  • Pick an official 3-letter in-game Tag (e.g. [KNT], [APX], [SHD]).
  • Tag is displayed on in-game nametags, HUD, and live match killfeeds!
🎵
Branding
3. Logo & Walk-In Music
  • 🎨 Team Logo: Pick your squad mascot (Lion, Dragon, Eagle, etc.) for live scoreboards & broadcasts.
  • 🎵 Custom Intro Music: Select your squad's signature hype walk-in music that plays dynamically on arena entry!
Match Types
Arena Game Modes
Four battle formats engineered for 5v5 team coordination and individual skill.
🚩
5v5 Strategy
Capture The Flag (CTF)
  • Infiltrate the enemy base, steal their flag, and sprint home.
  • Carrier lockout: Flag carriers cannot shoot — your squad must provide suppressive cover fire!
⚔️
Squad Shootout
Team Deathmatch (TDM)
  • Squad eliminations with instant tactical cover building and weapon crate looting.
  • A shrinking electric storm perimeter forces climactic final showdowns.
👑
Zone Control
King of the Hill (KOTH)
  • Dominate a shifting central capture hill against heavy enemy assault.
  • Hold the zone under fire and deploy barricades to secure points per second.
🚚
Objective Assault
Payload & Domination
  • Payload: Escort a mobile cargo cart into enemy territory while defenders contest.
  • Domination: Coordinate squad splits to capture and maintain 3 key control points.

Game Mechanics
Tactical Combat Features
Master these core mechanics to outplay opponents and lead your team to victory.
🧱
Real-Time Cover
Instant Tactical Building
  • Deploy wooden barricades, walls, and ramps in real-time under fire.
  • Outmaneuver opponents by taking high ground or blocking enemy sightlines.
⚡
Dynamic Perimeter
Contracting Storm Perimeter
  • An electric storm ring shrinks steadily towards the center of the arena.
  • Forces squads into intense final-circle battles with zero stalling.
🤖
Training Range
AI Sentry Bot Practice
  • Intelligent AI bots that loot crates, jump obstacles, and shoot in squads.
  • Warm up your aim and practice squad coordination before official fixtures.
📊
Live Analytics
In-Game Scoreboard & Ratings
  • Real-time scoreboards tracking K/D, captures, and player ratings.
  • Live spectator cameras, team overlays, and automatic killfeed broadcasts.
